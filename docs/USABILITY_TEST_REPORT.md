## 3. Deep-Dive Evaluation with 5 Synthetic Trader Family Archetypes

### Archetype 1: Sharma Kirana & General Store (Khari Baoli, Old Delhi)
- **Profile:** Lala Omprakash Sharma (62 yrs), sole proprietor of 40-year grocery wholesale shop. Wife, 2 sons (one store manager, one tech professional in Bengaluru), 1 married daughter in Jaipur.
- **Key Assets:** ₹1.8 Cr shop, ₹1.2 Cr ancestral home, ₹45 Lakh SBI FDs, ₹52 Lakh gold, ₹18 Lakh supplier udhaari.
- **Identified Pitfalls & Verification:**
  1. *Nominee Mismatch:* SBI FDs listed younger son Amit as bank nominee, but will allocated 60% to mother and 40% to daughter. Platform flagged conflict and cited *Sarbati Devi* precedent.
  2. *Ancestral Coparcenary:* Daryaganj home had ancestral roots; platform flagged married daughter's equal birthright under Sec 6 HSA.

### Archetype 2: Gupta Cloth Merchant & Sons (Surat, Gujarat)
- **Profile:** Mohanlal Gupta (68 yrs), 50% partner in textile wholesale firm. Elderly mother (89 yrs), wife, partner son, doctor daughter.
- **Key Assets:** ₹2.4 Cr partnership capital, ₹1.5 Cr textile godown, ₹85 Lakh Demat, ₹35 Lakh CC limit.
- **Identified Pitfalls & Verification:**
  1. *Mother as Class I Statutory Heir:* In intestate succession, elderly mother inherits an equal 1/4th share.
  2. *Partnership Dissolution under Sec 42:* Platform warned of automatic dissolution upon death without an executed partnership addendum.

### Archetype 3: Verma Auto Spares & Hardware (Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh)
- **Profile:** Rameshwar Verma (65 yrs), auto parts distributor. Predeceased son's widow (Sunidhi) & minor grandson (Harsh, 9 yrs), younger son Deepak.
- **Key Assets:** ₹1.1 Cr showroom, ₹60 Lakh residential plot, ₹30 Lakh education FD for minor grandson.
- **Identified Pitfalls & Verification:**
  1. *Representation Rights (Per Stirpes):* Under Section 8 and Section 10 Rule 3 of the Hindu Succession Act, children and widow of a predeceased son inherit the exact share their deceased father would have taken, as **Class I statutory heirs**. The legal engine correctly allocates the deceased son's full 1/3rd branch to Sunidhi and Harsh.
  2. *Testamentary Minor Guardianship:* Platform prompted appointing Sunidhi as testamentary guardian for the minor's ₹30 Lakh FD.

### Archetype 4: Agarwal Sweets & Namkeen (Jaipur, Rajasthan)
- **Profile:** Kailash Chand Agarwal (71 yrs), confectionery brand with 2 branches. Wife, 2 sons managing branches, 1 married daughter in Delhi.
- **Key Assets:** ₹2.0 Cr registered trademark/goodwill, ₹2.2 Cr flagship sweet shop, ₹1.5 Cr farmland & dairy unit.
- **Identified Pitfalls & Verification:**
  1. *Brand Split Conflict:* Brand goodwill shared equally between sons; platform inserted non-compete and co-brand management clauses.
  2. *Farmland Succession:* Platform correctly allocated farmland to daughter Ananya, preventing partition litigation.

### Archetype 5: Patel Electronics & Mobile Care (Ahmedabad, Gujarat)
- **Profile:** Jayantilal Patel (59 yrs), electronics retail showroom. Daughter handles shop accounting and online sales, son studying in Canada.
- **Key Assets:** ₹1.75 Cr showroom, ₹1.1 Cr residential apartment, ₹65 Lakh equity portfolio.
- **Identified Pitfalls & Verification:**
  1. *Commercial Continuity Allocation:* Daughter bequeathed primary commercial showroom because she actively manages operations; son receives Demat portfolio. Platform provided clear justification clauses to prevent bias challenges.
