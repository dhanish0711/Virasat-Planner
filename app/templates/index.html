4 Essential Legal Rules for Family Businesses & Joint Families:

🏦 Bank Nominee ≠ Legal Owner

Under Supreme Court precedent (Sarbati Devi), a nominee is only a temporary trustee. Final legal ownership passes strictly via the Will or succession law.

⚖️ Equal Coparcenary for Daughters

Under the 2005 HSA amendment & Vineeta Sharma ruling, daughters possess birthright coparcenary rights equal to sons in ancestral property, even after marriage.

🏢 Partnership Firm Protection

Under Section 42(c) of the Indian Partnership Act, a firm automatically dissolves upon a partner's demise unless the deed includes an explicit succession clause.

🏛️ Sub-Registrar (SRO) Registration

While an un-registered will is legally valid with 2 witnesses, registering at the Sub-Registrar office prevents fraudulent dispute challenges.
